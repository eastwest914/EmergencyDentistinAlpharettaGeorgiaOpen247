Emergency Dentistry FAQ

What is ER dentistry?

ER dentistry means urgent dental care for sudden problems like severe pain, infection, broken teeth and dental injuries, available outside normal office hours.


Can you do a root canal the same day?

When a root canal is needed, we aim to start treatment as soon as possible, often the same day, depending on your exam and situation.


Do I need an appointment?

Please call first. We will tell you what to do, prepare for your arrival and get you in as quickly as we can.


Are you open on weekends and holidays?

Yes. We are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.


How can I pay?

We accept cash and credit cards. We will review costs with you before treatment.
